# Code of Conduct

TaskRuntime's community spaces should be useful, respectful, and welcoming.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks
- give specific, constructive feedback
- respect different experience levels
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disruption
- protect private or security-sensitive information

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner.
